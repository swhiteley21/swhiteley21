Landing, and getting gloriously lost

07
21 May

The ten-hour walking tour

  • Kyoto walking tour, all ten hours of it. "I only learn to be content."
  • Conveyor-belt sushi at £7.50, and a free folder won for eating enough plates (it became very useful).
  • North / south / east / west chimes at the robot. Tanuki statues and their famously large anatomy.
  • Lessons learnt: chewing gum sucks, Uniqlo umbrellas are kak, and Pocari Sweat is made by a medical company.
  • Kinkaku-ji, the golden temple, where the gold leaf is cleaned every night between 3am and 6am. Pikachu, the "lightning mouse". "Ah-mm", A-to-Z protection.
  • Kiyomizu-dera: "let's jump off the stage" meaning let's make a big decision. Drank the spring water, and crawled through the wishing hole to let go of bad people and situations.
  • Geiko-san vs maiko-san explained. Ramen with noodles to finish, while David left us for a foot massage.

22 May

Tea ceremony day

  • Matcha in the morning. Scott went for a roasted iced matcha.
  • Tea ceremony: two scoops of matcha (2g) to 70ml of water at 80–90°C. The full ceremony runs two to four hours.
  • Katsu curry with the zen man, a clean 10/10. Beno earned mad points.
  • Split up for Nishiki market, then large-intestine skewers and beets in a tiny bar.
  • Went delirious hunting for a restaurant, and Dave and Ben nearly broke up over it.

10
24 May

Hiroshima day trip

  • Shinkansen to Hiroshima.
  • The tour: the bomb, "Little Boy", detonated 600m above the ground. A typhoon swept away the radiation, leaving the land habitable within two months.
  • The land was mostly man-made, dug out from the mountains and largely flat, which is why it was chosen, with mountains behind to ricochet the blast for maximum effect.
  • Walked the town, ate okonomiyaki (the egg pancake).
  • Shinkansen back to Osaka, a walk around Dotonbori, then all-you-can-eat wagyu where we very nearly died.

25 May

Retro Osaka

  • Woke up at a reasonable hour for once. Train to Nakazakicho, old-town Osaka with retro charm.
  • Brunch at an eclectic spot: French toast, egg sando and an "English breakfast".
  • Thrifting through the streets, then a 10/10 matcha frappe at Meiz, with a Google Translate conversation with the matcha man. Bought our core good-luck man.
  • Octopus stone at Osaka Castle, and Kendo on display, the bamboo-sword sport with the fencing-style helmet.
  • Walked the longest shopping arcade in Japan.
  • A hole-in-the-wall dandan noodle joint with Japanese rock music, and a secret serving of risotto made from the leftover broth.
